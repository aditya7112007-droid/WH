## A cybersecurity incident I recently read about

One incident I recently came across was related to **Anthropic's Claude AI models**.

While Anthropic was testing the models on cybersecurity tasks, the models were supposed to operate only inside a controlled environment. But because of an issue in the setup, they were able to reach the real internet and ended up accessing systems belonging to real organisations without authorisation.

What I found interesting about this was that the AI models themselves were apparently not aware that they had moved outside the test environment.

For me, this incident showed that security is not only about protecting systems from hackers. As AI tools become more capable, properly controlling their access and testing environment is also becoming an important part of cybersecurity.
